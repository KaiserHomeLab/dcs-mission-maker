DCS Mission Maker

Six quick steps. Drop the finished .miz into Saved Games\DCS\Missions.

🛈

⚙ Settings

1 Where are you flying?

Pick the map, the airfield you fly from, and the enemy area you're heading for.

2 What's the mission?

Loadouts adjust automatically to the mission you pick.

3 Who's flying?

One card per flight. Each player seat is one person; AI wingmen fill the rest of the flight. Callsigns are real DCS callsigns, so AWACS and ATC call you by name.

4 Friendly AI & support

AI flights fly alongside you: fighters on CAP, escorts that stick to your flight, SEAD that goes in ahead of you, and strike or CAS packages. Delay a launch to time it with your push.

Support

5 Conditions & options

The year is set from your aircraft's era.

Goes on the comm card, kneeboards and Discord post for this mission. Leave it empty to use the default from the server settings. Don't put the SRS password here; say where to find it instead.

6 Scripts & mods

Popular community scripts, embedded in the mission. Libraries they need are added for you. Put your own .lua files in the scripts folder and they show up here too.

Mods your group has

Only tick mods everyone has installed. Mod aircraft show up in the aircraft list, and the mission tells DCS which mods are required.

Mission summary

Auto picks the era most of your seats belong to. Enemy aircraft, ground units, air defenses and support all follow the era.
